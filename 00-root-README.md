# PaySmart Nigeria — Digital Payment Platform
### A Business Analysis Capstone Project | Group 6

This repository documents the end-to-end business analysis for **PaySmart**, a proposed USSD- and app-based digital payment platform designed to bring 40+ million cash-dependent informal traders in Nigeria into the formal financial system.

It's organized as a live case study — from problem discovery through requirements, process redesign, financial justification, quality/risk planning, and post-launch measurement — using the same artifacts a business analyst would produce on a real fintech engagement.

## The Problem

Millions of Nigerian market traders operate entirely in cash. That means:
- **~7 minutes lost per transaction** on price haggling, cash counting, and hunting for change
- **No transaction history**, so traders can't prove income and stay locked out of business loans
- Existing mobile banking apps assume smartphones, internet access, and literacy levels most rural/peri-urban traders don't have

**The ask:** design a payment platform that works on *any* mobile phone, with no internet required, simple enough for low-literacy users, that generates the transaction history traders need to qualify for credit.

## The Proposed Solution

| Channel | What it does |
|---|---|
| **USSD (\*111#)** | Works on any phone, no internet — dial, enter seller ID and amount, confirm with PIN |
| **Android Lite App** | Large buttons, icons, and voice guidance in English, Hausa, Igbo, Yoruba, and Pidgin |
| **Seller Dashboard** | Web portal for transaction history, exportable reports, and a credit-worthiness score |
| **Business Loan Gateway** | Micro-loans from partner microfinance banks, underwritten by transaction data instead of payslips |

## Headline Results

- **86% reduction** in average transaction time (7 minutes → ~1 minute)
- **90% reduction** in daily reconciliation time for sellers
- **61.87% 3-year ROI**, with a **2.8-year payback period** on a ₦160M total investment
- Projected to reach **10,387 monthly active traders** and **₦318.5M in Gross Transaction Value** by month 12
- **98.1% transaction success rate**, against a 95% target

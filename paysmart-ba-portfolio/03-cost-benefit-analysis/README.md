# 03 — 3-Year Cost-Benefit Analysis

**Technique:** Cost-benefit analysis with ROI and payback period modeling, built across one-time costs, recurring costs, and multi-stream revenue projections

[Cost-Benefit Analysis](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1HnETuP6UEYl-s2aixtq-6tkOURtzB7kJ87Y-4T3tVrY/edit?usp=drivesdk)

## Objective
Answer the question every sponsor and investor asks before funding a project: is this worth it, and when do we see a return?

## 1. One-Time Costs (Year 1 only)

| Cost Item | Year 1 (₦) |
|---|---|
| Platform development (USSD + App + Dashboard) | 45,000,000 |
| CBN licensing and regulatory compliance | 12,000,000 |
| Marketing and trader onboarding | 18,000,000 |
| Infrastructure (servers, SMS gateway) | 10,000,000 |
| **Total One-Time** | **85,000,000** |

## 2. Recurring Costs (per year, Years 1–3)

| Cost Item | Per Year (₦) | 3-Year Total (₦) |
|---|---|---|
| Server hosting and maintenance | 8,000,000 | 24,000,000 |
| Customer support team (10 staff) | 12,000,000 | 36,000,000 |
| SMS costs | 5,000,000 | 15,000,000 |
| **Total Recurring** | **25,000,000** | **75,000,000** |

**Total 3-year cost: ₦160,000,000**

## 3. Revenue Model (Benefits)

| Benefit Item | Assumption | Year 1 (₦) | Year 2 (₦) | Year 3 (₦) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Transaction fee revenue | 0.5% per transaction, 500k tx/month by end of Y1 | 18,000,000 | 54,000,000 | 108,000,000 |
| Loan origination fee | 2% per micro-loan, launched Year 2 | 0 | 15,000,000 | 28,000,000 |
| Premium seller dashboard | ₦2,000/month, 5,000 sellers by Y2 | 0 | 12,000,000 | 24,000,000 |
| **Total Benefit** | | **18,000,000** | **81,000,000** | **160,000,000** |

**Total 3-year benefit: ₦259,000,000**

## 4. Net Benefit by Year

| Year | Net Benefit (₦) |
|---|---|
| Year 1 | -92,000,000 |
| Year 2 | +56,000,000 |
| Year 3 | +135,000,000 |
| **3-Year Total** | **+99,000,000** |

## 5. ROI & Payback Period

```
ROI = (Total Benefits − Total Costs) / Total Costs × 100
    = (₦259,000,000 − ₦160,000,000) / ₦160,000,000 × 100
    = 61.87%
```
→ For every ₦100 invested, ₦161.87 is returned over 3 years.

```
Payback Period = Total Investment / Annual Net Benefit
               = ₦160,000,000 / ₦56,000,000 (Year 2 net benefit used as benchmark)
               = 2.8 years (2 years, 10 months, 3 days)
```

## Why this approach
The model is deliberately honest about Year 1 running at a loss (-₦92M) while user acquisition ramps up, rather than smoothing the numbers to look positive immediately. Showing the dip — and exactly which two revenue streams (loans, subscriptions) don't activate until Year 2 — is what makes the case credible to a finance stakeholder who will stress-test the assumptions.

# 05 — KPI Dashboard

**Technique:** KPI definition and target-setting, 12-month performance simulation, and RAG (Red/Amber/Green) status scorecard reporting

## Objective
Define, upfront, how success would actually be measured after launch — then track performance against those targets rather than reporting numbers with no benchmark.

## 1. Monthly Trend Data (simulated, 12 months)

| Month | Active Traders | GTV (₦M) | Success Rate | Satisfaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jan | 312 | 8.4 | 91.2% | 3.6/5.0 |
| Apr | 3,102 | 78.6 | 95.3% | 4.0/5.0 |
| Jul | 7,108 | 187.3 | 96.8% | 4.2/5.0 |
| Oct | 9,112 | 267.4 | 97.6% | 4.4/5.0 |
| Dec | 10,387 | 318.5 | 98.1% | 4.5/5.0 |

Traders grew from 312 to 10,387 over the year — a trajectory that shows steady, compounding adoption rather than a single launch spike, which matters for a sponsor validating that word-of-mouth/community onboarding actually works.

## 2. KPI Scorecard (Month 12 status)

| KPI | Target | Result | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly Active Traders | 10,000 by Month 12 | 10,387 | 🟢 Green |
| Transaction Success Rate | ≥95% | 98.1% | 🟢 Green |
| Gross Transaction Value | ₦500M by Month 12 | ₦318.5M | 🔴 Red |
| Trader Satisfaction Score | ≥4.0/5.0 by Month 6 | 4.0/5.0 | 🟢 Green |
| System Uptime | ≥99.9% | 99.95% | 🟢 Green |
| Users Building Verifiable Financial Footprint | ≥70% of active merchants | ≥71% | 🟢 Green |
| Security Incident Rate | 0 critical, ≤3 minor/year | 0 critical, 2 minor | 🟢 Green |
| Stakeholder Satisfaction | ≥95% | 96% | 🟢 Green |

## 3. Reading the Red Status
GTV missed its ₦500M target despite trader count beating its target — meaning traders signed up faster than they transacted. That gap points to a specific next action: drive **transaction volume per active trader**, not just registrations (e.g. incentivizing repeat use, expanding merchant categories, or reducing per-transaction friction further).

## Why this approach
A dashboard where every KPI is green isn't trustworthy — it either means the targets were set too low, or the reporting is being softened. Flagging GTV honestly, and pairing it with a specific hypothesis for why it lagged, is what makes a KPI report a decision-making tool rather than a status update.
